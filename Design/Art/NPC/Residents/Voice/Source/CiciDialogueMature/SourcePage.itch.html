A tiny pack of voice clips for use with VNs and dating sim type games. You're free to use them in anything you like, though!

I'm now open to suggestions for future projects/improvements upon current ones! Please send me an email <cicifyre@gmail.com> or  use the contact form on my current homepage.

  • 57 WAV (32-bit) sound files total. 
  • 6 greetings (hi, bye)
  • how, what, when, where, why (unique recordings 3 times each)
  • short replies (thanks, okay)
  • small talk (how are you, i'm fine thank you)
  • one confession and two rejection lines
  • misc noises like gasping and ah, eh, uh...

-------------

Wasn't sure if to call it "mature female" or "tomboy", but I had a different kind of voice in mind for tomboy... So I went with mature.

For this series, I am working on a better organization method. It is possible I re-record this pack and the previous "bright female" pack, too. I plan to add some more clips, but exactly when I'm not certain at this time.
