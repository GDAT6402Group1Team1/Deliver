I felt like testing a new microphone and decided to make a tiny pack of voice clips for use with VNs and dating sim type games. You're free to use them in anything you like, though!

I'm now open to suggestions for future projects/improvements upon current ones! Please send me an email <cicifyre@gmail.com> or  use the contact form on my current homepage.

  • 72 WAV (32-bit) sound files total. 
  • Around 20 ~ 24 lines with 1 ~ 3 intonations.
  • Basic greetings: good morning, afternoon, evening, hello, etc.
  • One word responses: How? What? Why? Okay.
  • One confession and one rejection line in three intonations.
  • Three laughter clips.
