Dialogue Sfx

A downloadable asset pack

Dialogue sfx for cute characters. The short sfxs are best used as randomized pitches for Animal Crossing-like voices. Emote-like noise are included for impactful moments. Sounds include:

1. Ah Reaction  (AHHHHHH)

2. Ah Short  (Ah)

3. Garble Long (Noises)

4. Garble Short 1 (Scribble) 

5. Garble Short 2 (Scrabble)

6. Honk (Honk)

7. Huh Long  (Huuuuh)

8. Huh Short (huh)

9. Me (The cutest one)

10. Meh (Low and Bass-y)

11. Retro Ascend (High Pitch)

12. Retro Descend (Even Higher Pitch)

13. Retro Double (Oops!)

14. Retro Low (How low can ya go)

15. Who (A little ghost questioning your authority)

All Sounds in this pack are free to use in projects with credit.
